# Learn computational drug discovery

Understand the physics, chemistry, and computational methods behind molecular
drug discovery through three connected tracks.

| Track | Question | Entry point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A — Molecular physics and simulation** | How does the physical model work? | [Physics curriculum](physics/README.md) |
| **B — Chemical structure and properties** | What is the molecule, and what state is it in? | [Chemistry curriculum](chemistry/README.md) |
| **C — Structure-based drug discovery** | What does the physics mean for a drug? | [Drug-discovery curriculum](drug_discovery/README.md) |

They meet in the provenance-led
[imatinib–BCR-ABL1 capstone](capstone_imatinib.md).

## Track A — Molecular physics and simulation

Start with units and particles. Build energy, force, integration, molecular
mechanics, periodic systems, solvent, electrostatics, and free energy.

[Open the physics curriculum](physics/README.md)

## Track B — Chemistry

Build molecular identity, representation, descriptors, protonation,
stereochemistry, polarity, hydrogen bonding, and ADME trade-off reasoning.

[Open the chemistry curriculum](chemistry/README.md)

## Track C — Drug discovery

Work with protein structures, binding-site geometry, interactions, docking,
pose validation, interaction fingerprints, SAR, and the bridge from structural
observations to thermodynamics.

[Open the drug-discovery curriculum](drug_discovery/README.md)

## Suggested order

1. Track A, sections 1–10, with the [physics deep dives](physics/README.md) as
   needed.
2. Track B, modules 01–08.
3. Track A, sections 11–24.
4. Track C, modules 01–10.
5. The [capstone](capstone_imatinib.md), including the final scientific audit.

For the longer rationale, see the [CADD overview](overview.md). For the detailed
prerequisite and module-status tables, see the
[curriculum reference](curriculum.md).
